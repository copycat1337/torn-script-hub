GHOST PROTOCOL – WAR TACTICS

THIS WEEK'S WAR TEMPLATE: 24H

⏱ WHAT DOES 24H MEAN?

This war uses the 24-hour battle template.

Unlike our usual 3-day wars, this battle will last only 24 hours. When the timer runs out, the faction with the highest score wins.

This means we have just ONE day to make our points count.

Every point we gain and every point we lose matters much more than usual.

We need to attack aggressively, protect our score and avoid giving away unnecessary points.

OUR BATTLE TACTICS

Our main strategy will be to attack and fly, or attack and take Ipecac to protect ourselves from counterattacks.

We cannot afford to sit around waiting for opponents to attack us. We need to make points quickly and avoid losing them unnecessarily.

✈️ ATTACK AND FLY

If you're busy, have finished attacking or don't want to risk being hit, fly out after your attacks.

This is especially important during a 24H war because we may not have enough time for our bounty hunter to claim bounties and protect offline members.

Ipecac is another option when you want to remain available without making yourself an easy target.

👥 GROUP ATTACKS

When opponents are online, we should try to coordinate group attacks rather than fighting them individually.

Our approach:

  • One member pins down the strongest opponent, keeping them occupied.
  • The remaining members focus on scoring points as quickly as possible.
  • Coordinate attacks and avoid wasting energy on unnecessary fights.
  • Once the target is dealt with, regroup and move to the next opponent.

Communication and timing will make a huge difference.

TEMPORARY WEAPONS

Use temporary weapons to make opponents miss attacks and give yourself an advantage during fights.

Weapon Best Use
Flash Grenade Use to reduce opponent accuracy and make them miss more attacks.
Tear Gas Use when an opponent is hitting you too often. Helps reduce their Dexterity for longer.
Pepper Spray Best against weaker opponents. Similar effect to Tear Gas, but lasts for a shorter time.

IMPORTANT: TEAR GAS VS PEPPER SPRAY

Tear Gas: Better when fighting opponents who are hitting you too frequently. Its longer-lasting effect makes it useful in fights that take more time.

Pepper Spray: Has a similar effect but only lasts a short time. Best used against opponents with lower stats than your own, where you can take advantage of the short window.

Choose the right temporary weapon depending on your opponent and the situation.

ACTIVITY WILL WIN THIS WAR

This is where things get different from our usual wars.

Normally, we have around 3 days to fight. Being active for 10 hours across those 3 days gives us plenty of opportunities to make points.

This time, we only have 24 hours.

Being active for 3 hours during this war is proportionally the same as being active for 10 hours during a 3-day war.

That means every hour online is more valuable.

  • Please try to be as active as possible during the war.
  • Take advantage of opportunities to attack.
  • Coordinate with other members when opponents are online.
  • Don't give away points through unnecessary counterattacks.

🌍 GOING OFFLINE? FLY BEFORE THE WAR!

If you know you will be busy IRL or unable to participate, please fly before the war starts.

Our preferred locations:

JAPAN · UAE · AFRICA

IMPORTANT: Before flying, check whether another faction member is already at your destination.

We want to spread out our members instead of having large groups in the same location.

This makes it harder for opponents to target multiple offline members in one place.

Attack fast, work together, protect your points and stay active.

And remember: if you're going to be offline, fly out before the war starts!
